GFOS Imager

Writes a GFOS base image to a USB drive, NVMe or SD card. The base image starts your machine; GFOS fetches the rest once it's running.

Test build, not for release.

3Settings for the card (optional)

So the card joins your network at its first start. Leave them empty and the card asks on its own screen instead.

They go onto the card as a file it reads once; the card takes the passwords out after using them. They aren't kept on this computer.
